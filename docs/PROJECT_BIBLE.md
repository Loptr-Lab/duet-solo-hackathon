# Loptr Lab project bible: the Weaver to Veiled Dominion

**Status:** Orientation and research reference, 2026-09-26. This document does not create game rules, a collaboration agreement, or narrative canon.

**Pitch order:** DUET is the current ID@Xbox lead because it offers a playable, accessible artifact. Veiled Dominion is the larger four-player destination and rules authority. PIXIE data and integrations are separate work, not a prerequisite for pitching or building the playable DUET experience. This pitch priority does not alter any game's canon.

## Event and decision sequence

1. **Originating proposal:** sent privately. The public Weaver door documents its existence without publishing correspondence.
2. **The Weaver:** frames two histories and the translation problem. An invited collaborator may add their own account; silence or a review comment is not acceptance.
3. **Duet:** playable accessibility and mechanics experiment where two sides meet in play.
4. **52 Cards of War:** proposed deck and kingdom layer. Open design questions include arbitration, cooperative combinations, pip progression, and the relation of personal decks to the shared deck.
5. **Veiled Dominion:** four-player rules authority. A Duet or deck experiment enters canon only after an explicit decision in that repository.

These are relationships between artifacts, not a claim that each proposal was accepted or that the historical designs shipped in this sequence.

## How to use 52 Aces

The [reading list](https://52aces.com/reading-list/) is curated by **Ace Eddleman**. Cite the list as an external discovery source, then read and cite the underlying book or paper when making a substantive design claim. Suggested starting points:

| Question in this project | Reading-list section and examples | Practical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How do two people learn each other's systems? | Learning & Memory: *Make It Stick*, *Thinking in Systems* | Frame study sessions, feedback, and handoffs; test ideas independently. |
| How do asymmetric sides compete or cooperate? | Strategy, Competition & Games: *Characteristics of Games*, *Uncertainty in Games*, *Finite & Infinite Games* | Inform prototypes for the unresolved Weaver design issues. |
| How do incentives and power shape collaboration? | Capitalism; Con Artists & Fraud; Research | Ask clearer authorship, incentive, information, and consent questions without treating analogy as evidence about a person. |
| How does a fictional world stay open to interpretation? | Philosophy and Fiction | Creative prompts only; preserve the separate narrative and game canon sources. |

The 52 Aces page is not a substitute for rights clearance, peer review, or contributor consent. Do not copy its bibliography wholesale into a game or imply that Ace Eddleman participated in this project.

## Authority and privacy rules

- This bible points to sources; it never overrides [the Weaver door](provenance/WEAVER_DOOR.md), [Duet's divergence register](CANONICAL_DIVERGENCES.md), or [Veiled Dominion's canon policy](https://github.com/Loptr-Lab/veiled-dominion-engine/blob/main/docs/CANON_AND_CONTINUITY.md).
- Mark a statement as documented, proposed, experimental, narrative canon, current game canon, historical, or unresolved before moving it between layers.
- Preserve independent memories and private correspondence separately. Public reading does not grant a public response pathway.
- PIXIE may explain boundaries and organize sourced context; its presence on a page does not authenticate a collaborator, send an invitation, or make a decision for a person.
