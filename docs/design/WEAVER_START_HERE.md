# Start Here — The Weaver

This is the entry point into your side of the project. Everything below already exists in this repo or the public site — this doc just compiles it in one place so you're not hunting for it.

## Where things stand

Two decks exist in this world: hers (built out in full) and yours (sketched in concept, not yet built into a deck). Duet is where the two of you play each other. Once united, you play together as one kingdom — the 52 Cards of War — against outside kingdoms. When the two of you hit an impasse, you settle it with a simple game of War, deck against deck.

## What's already in the repo for you

- **`docs/design/dueling-systems.md`** — the full design handoff. Covers how the two decks settle conflicts (a game of War), how they cooperate, how your deck relates to the base "52 Cards of War," and what the card numbers (2–10) could mean beyond raw combat value. Also has the full roster for her deck (the Sufi Tariqah variant) for reference, and everything currently known about yours (the Systems Quartet).
- **`docs/design/prompts-for-the-weaver-systems-quartet.md`** — three prompts written for you to run through your own Claude session, to help you think through (1) what kind of 2-player game your side actually is, and (2) how to convert the Systems Quartet from a board variant into an actual card deck. These are scaffolding, not a spec — nothing in them decides your deck's content for you.
- **`loptrlab.com/rules.html`** — the public-facing rules page. Grounds the win-condition philosophy and the Marriage of Lady Violet origin scene, if you want the story context before diving into mechanics.

## What's already yours, not blank space

The Systems Quartet (Cannon, Coulter, Veblen, Fresco) already exists as real design work, built from a reading list you gave — full mechanics, full board aesthetic ("The Laboratory"). That's not something anyone invented for you; it's already your material. What's genuinely still open is converting it into card form — suits, ranks, face cards — so it can actually be played in a game of War against her deck.

## The bigger shape this feeds into

Two 4-player modes exist as concepts: **World War** (AI-driven) and **So You Wanna Rule the World** (human vs. 4 other human teams). In the human mode, each player's powers take shape around their own deck — meaning your deck, once built, isn't just for playing against her. It's a template for how any player's persona gets built in that mode. Paragon's card-based hero system is the closest existing reference point for that idea, with a caution: its card-lock-in was also its biggest source of community complaints, worth designing around rather than repeating.

## Not your problem to solve alone

Deck balance, mechanics tuning — none of that has to be carried solo. Say so whenever something needs more hands.
