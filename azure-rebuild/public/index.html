    Rules quick guide

    Any piece ending a turn adjacent (including diagonally) to the enemy Rebirth becomes Veiled unless protected by its own Death. A Veiled piece may move one empty square straight forward and cannot capture. If your Rebirth ends your turn Veiled, you lose. Death cannot be captured. Pawns promote to Rebirth if you have none; otherwise they promote to a standard queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

    Prototype boundary: this is local play only. Remote rooms, identity, durable saves, AT Protocol sign-in, and Azure deployment are not implemented by this preview.
