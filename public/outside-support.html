Outside Support

You can reach people outside DUET without explaining anything to PIXIE.

  • For immediate support in the United States, 988 Lifeline offers call, text, and chat options. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
  • For peer meetings about technology use, see Internet and Technology Addicts Anonymous (ITAA).

These are independent resources. DUET and Loptr Lab are not affiliated with them.

Opening another service can leave a record in your browser, phone, messages, or with that service. DUET has no support-use analytics; hosting request logs may still record page visits.
