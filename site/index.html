Accessibility-first game research

Veiled Dominion: Duet

A screen-reader-first strategy-game experiment exploring accessible play, bounded game help, and the design of information in a world shaped by fog and hidden knowledge.

The playable game is currently offline.

This page is the project's public information page only. Gameplay, rooms, matchmaking, and game services are not available here.

What this project explores

Accessible strategy-game interaction designed for blind and low-vision players first, with attention to screen-reader feedback, keyboard operation, orientation, and recovery.

PIXIE's role

PIXIE is bounded game-help support: fixed answers and a neutral fallback. It is not an autonomous player, decision-maker, or authority over a human player's choices.

Research boundary

Duet is a separate two-player experimental mechanics lab. Its experimental rules do not automatically become canon for the wider Veiled Dominion setting.
