2027 hands-free test cases · evidence checked November 3, 2026

One goal for Break This Build 2027

Test whether a player can start, play, recover, and finish First Movement and Duet using hands-free input. MouthPad and Augmental VOX are separate test cases for each game. Ordinary pointer/keyboard checks help find barriers; they do not prove device compatibility.

Augmental's VOX page and linked chess demonstration motivate the experiment, not a compatibility claim. Checked November 3, 2026: Augmental describes MouthPad cursor/click control and VOX dictation through macOS/iOS. The inspected VOX documentation does not establish browser game-key mapping or sustained key-down/key-up behavior. Do not name a supported VOX game setup until the exact app, OS, mapping, event delivery, repeats, and holds are verified. iPadOS support must be confirmed separately from the vendor's iOS description.

  • First Movement · MouthPad — blocked: no physical device available. Ordinary one-pointer Chromium proxy tested Start, direction/Jump as separate clicks, missed-ledge recovery, Pause/Resume, five-fragment finish and Restart. Holding the original direction control captured the pointer and prevented reaching Jump with that same pointer. The reversible click-to-move option latches a direction; click again to stop. Pause, mode changes, and page interruption clear movement. Physical fit, fatigue and reliable control remain untested.
  • First Movement · VOX — blocked: hardware and verified game-key mapping/hold behavior unavailable. First verify discrete Start, Pause, Resume and Jump actions. Only then test movement plus jump timing. Optional timing assist was checked with ordinary keys; it supplies the beat boost and labels assisted runs, which are excluded from the best-score record. It does not establish a playable VOX route.
  • Duet · MouthPad — blocked: no physical device available. Ordinary single-pointer proxy tested a local two-player session, same-square selection cancellation, legal moves, turn handoff, a 34-move finish and restart. Both seats were operated by automation; independent two-player participation remains planned. Test setup, fatigue, recovery and the hands-free player's control separately on the actual device.
  • Duet · VOX — blocked: hardware and verified browser key delivery unavailable. Ordinary keyboard proxy tested selection, confirmation, Escape cancellation, turn handoff and a full local session. Repeated held Enter must not submit a second action or select the previous player's piece. Fresh repeated voice commands may differ from KeyboardEvent.repeat and need their own device check.

Separate evidence tracks: ordinary pointer/keyboard — tested in automated Linux Chromium 154 at tablet width; ordinary physical iPad Safari — blocked, no device; physical MouthPad — blocked, no device; physical VOX — blocked, no device or verified game mapping; VoiceOver — blocked, no Apple accessibility test setup. No device acceptance is claimed. Quiet Beat is optional branch-only review work: where available, test Start, tap, tempo change and Stop; this does not merge it into First Movement.

Record each run: planned, tested, or blocked; date, URL and build/commit; browser/version, OS, physical device and firmware/app; input/mapping and hold/repeat behavior; reproduction steps; expected versus actual result; help used, setup time, rest breaks, fatigue/recovery observations volunteered by the player, and next action. Let a participant stop at any time; agree on recording and public evidence before testing. Never substitute a pointer proxy or VoiceOver result for MouthPad or VOX evidence.

Portfolio review and invited work are different paths

A prototype can show scope, design, execution, and correction. Unsolicited material leaves the artist free to decline or ignore it. Commissioned or invited collaboration needs an agreed brief, permissions, credit, scope, and any payment before work proceeds. Rhapsody's public repository and merged PR #13 show what was built; the inspected Rhapsody record does not establish private delivery, receipt, response, an invitation, or a commission. Do not infer a relationship from public availability.
