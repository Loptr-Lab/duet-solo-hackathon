Portfolio review and invited work are different paths

A prototype can show scope, design, execution, and correction. Unsolicited material leaves the artist free to decline or ignore it. Commissioned or invited collaboration needs an agreed brief, permissions, credit, scope, and any payment before work proceeds. Rhapsody's public repository and merged PR #13 show what was built; the inspected Rhapsody record does not establish private delivery, receipt, response, an invitation, or a commission. Do not infer a relationship from public availability.

Build one piece of portfolio evidence

Choose one small First Movement interaction or accessibility improvement, such as clearer Pause/Resume instructions. Write the problem and acceptance criteria, identify the starting commit, explain any AI assistance and your own decisions, show what you tested and corrected, and link the resulting code diff or demo. Record device/browser/input and distinguish automation from real-device testing. Use the existing portfolio section for the full exercise and optional training links. Training is separate from contributor approval, employment, or payment.
